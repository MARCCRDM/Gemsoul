# Skin Design Catalog — Production Sheets (v1 launch)

**Companion doc:** [skin-system-proposal.md](skin-system-proposal.md) owns channels, pricing, and tradeability. This doc owns the *designs*: what every skin actually looks like, piece by piece, ready for a builder. Concept art: `docs/skin-concepts/` in the Gemsoul repo (11 renders).

**Material vocabulary (Roblox):** Plastic (flat base), SmoothPlastic (clean premium), Metal (mechanical), DiamondPlate (tread), Neon (glow emissive), Glass (crystal), Granite/CorrodedMetal/Slate (texture for worn/rot looks). **Glow discipline:** every skin gets at most ONE Neon material zone (visor, emblem, or trim crack) — more than one glow and silhouettes get noisy at bracket distance.

## Line 8 — TRIM TOKENS · STORE · R$20-40 · the recolor system

A trim token recolors ONE zone on any owned line: Trim, Visor/Glow, or Emblem. Palette: any single hue from the game's approved swatch (12 hues incl. 4 element colors and holo). Rules: element hues are *fashion-only* on skins (gem glow still reveals true element); holo costs R$40, solid R$20. This is the impulse shelf and the long-tail revenue — it needs zero new geometry.

## SILHOUETTE RULES (all skins, hard constraints)

1. Family read survives: Prospector keeps lamp+pack, Skirmisher stays slim, Juggernaut stays wide. No skin may swap these signals.
2. One glow zone max (visor, emblem, OR trim) except Bossforged/Playtester which get one VFX each.
3. Element color on GEM sockets and ability VFX is never recolored by skins — scouting truth is sacred.
4. Every piece works with the OBS overlay and bracket inspect renders (no transparency-dependent reads).
5. Body shape never changes (no tall/petite in v1) — bracket identification is gameplay.
