# Viral clips: scripted scenes and the recruitment drive

How Saga Miners turns its sandbox into short, repeatable, shareable clips
(TikTok, Shorts, Reels), written 2026-10-26.

## What makes a clip travel

- **A hook in the first second.** A face-off, a punchline line of text, or
  a big flash. No slow intros.
- **One joke or one payoff.** The parley ends on a line; the HYPERBEAM ends
  on the sweep. One beat per clip.
- **Made to be repeated.** Players film the same setup with their own
  kaiju, skins and lines. That's how a format spreads; one-off moments
  don't.
- **Readable on a phone with the sound off.** Big captions, high contrast,
  the subject in the middle third.
- **The world takes itself seriously.** Miners vs. the rot is played
  straight (wartime posters, a parley across a table), and that is why
  it's funny.

## Formats

1. **The Parley** (miner vs. brainrot, across a table). Two chairs, a
   table, one gem between them. Script:

   > ROT: "So. You're a miner."
   > MINER: "I'm a miner."
   > ROT: "And I'm brainrot. Let's not make this weird."
   > ROT: "All I want... is your resources."
   >
   > Then one of four answers, each its own ending:
   > - "Never." → ROT: "Then we do this the brainrot way. TUNG. TUNG. TUNG."
   > - "Here, take a rock." → ROT: "...This is a regular rock." MINER: "It's a regular rock." ROT: "I respect the hustle."
   > - "My kaiju would like a word." → the team steps in, roars → ROT: "...I'll see myself out."
   > - "Join us. Defend the mines." → ROT: "Recruiting the rot? Bold. ...Is there dental?"

   Four endings means four clips from one set, and players argue over
   which is best. **To build next:** the table set by the Shelter's front
   ramp, a seated brainrot NPC, a seat for the player, the lines as bubbles
   over both heads (everyone near sees them, so a friend can film) and a
   side-on two-shot framing.
2. **Recruitment ads** (in-universe propaganda). Wartime-poster parodies
   of the miners' war on the rot, made for this generation's humour:
   - **WE NEED YOU / DEFEND OUR MINES**: a helmeted miner, gem badge,
     sunburst. "Enlist at the Shelter. Bring your kaiju."
   - **KILL THE ROT / IT WANTS YOUR GEMS**: a log-headed brainrot, crossed
     out. "No deals. No mercy. No rot."
   - **THE ROT WANTS YOUR GEMS / DON'T NEGOTIATE**: the parley table.
     "All it wants is your resources. Give it nothing." (This one ties the
     poster series to the Parley clips.)

   The posters are in `assets/images/propaganda/` (made by
   `tools/make_propaganda_posters.py`, 800×1200). A 15-second ad cut:
   - 0–2 s: black, then "THEY CAME THROUGH THE RIFT."
   - 2–6 s: critters pouring out of a pulsing tear.
   - 6–9 s: a miner swings, the kaiju team leaps into formation.
   - 9–12 s: the HYPERBEAM fires, sweeping from the boss to the swarm.
   - 12–15 s: the poster: "WE NEED YOU. DEFEND OUR MINES."
3. **Hyperbeam sweeps.** First person, the beam finds the biggest threat,
   then swings through the pack. Players clip their best sweeps.
4. **Robbed.** Getting killed, then a critter giggling off with your gem
   to its portal. Clip it, caption "the rot took my legendary."

## What the game gives creators

- The scenes are scripted and repeatable, so anyone can make the same
  clip.
- Posters in the world (next: up on the Shelter walls) double as
  backdrops.
- Brainrot characters are already in the game (critters, Rift bosses),
  so the lore holds up on screen.
