# Saga Miners roadmap (notes from 10/7/26)

Marc's planning notes, with a recommendation and current state for each item.
Nothing here is built yet; it's the agreed list to work through.

## 1. Levelling

- **Notes:** needs to be discussed and implemented.
- **Today:** there's no player level. Only the Season Path has XP (`Shared/Season`).
- **Recommendation:** one account level, earned from mining, fights, dungeons and events. Levels gate *access* (dungeon tiers, surge rarity caps, see 5), never raw stats. That keeps PvP decided by gear and skill.

## 2. Sub-classes and armor variety

- **Notes:** keep Prospector / Skirmisher / Juggernaut, or dissolve them for a bigger armor pool? Leaning towards keeping them and expanding the armor sets.
- **Recommendation:** keep them as armor *styles*, not classes, which is how they already work (everyone is a Warrior). Name armor without the style tag, and grow the pool by adding sets per style.

## 3. Content

- **PvP:** 1v1 only, which is fine. Polish the arena.
- **PvE Rivals (casual):** win and you earn coins; lose and you get nothing.
- **Dungeons:**
  - Elemental bosses at several difficulty levels, solo or in a party.
  - **The boss drop:** a pile of armor drops. You pick *one*, and the rest goes to the Market. This becomes the main armor source and replaces crafting; it's not meant to be a crafting grind.
- **Recommendation:** the drop-pile is the strongest idea here. It feeds the Market and gives every run a decision. Unclaimed pieces should be sold by the game at a fixed price that scales with rarity, so prices stay sane. Crafting gets retired or turned into repair and upgrade only.

## 4. Introduction flow

1. Start in the mining lobby.
2. Get directed to mine.
3. Gems and armor: socketing changes the armor.
4. Gems and surges: Q/E/R.
5. Basic combat (the training lesson already exists).
6. The Market.
7. Suggested additions:
   - the first dungeon (boss pick-one-armor moment)
   - Bring a Friend
   - daily and event board

## 5. Charge

- **Charge starts at 0** and is earned by attacking.
- **Today:** live combat starts at 100 (`Combat.luau`); the technology prototype starts at 40 with +3 per hit.
- **Surge cost scales with rarity.** Legendary surges should cost about 75–100% of the bar, so new players can't fire them, and choosing big-cost versus small-cost surges becomes a playstyle choice.
- **Armor and gem RNG buffs could include:**
  - charge regen %
  - +% charge per hit (X hits = Y%)
  - slower stamina regen (a downside roll)

## 6. Events and economy

- **Events:** timed dungeon or arena challenges. They're hard, but the loot is high.
- **"How do players get better if drops are the only armor source?"**
  - Gems sell to players on the Market at retail.
  - The in-game store buys anything, cheaper.
  - The boss-drop overflow (3) keeps armor supply on the Market.

## 7. Outreach

Show long-term commitment on the website and socials. The website should have an inquiry form for other Roblox studios about universe collaboration and interoperability, to build intrinsic and extrinsic value for GemSoul.

## 8. Showcase

A "Showcase" feature for displaying cosmetics, gear and gems, for example a profile pedestal in the lobby and inspect-a-player.

## Suggested build order

1. Charge rework (5): small, and it fixes balance now.
2. Levelling (1), so later systems can gate on it.
3. Boss drop-pile and Market supply (3 and 6), then retire crafting.
4. The intro flow (4) on top of those.
5. Events (6).
6. Showcase (8).
7. Armor set expansion (2), as ongoing content.
