# Gem surge expansion — 36 gem abilities

36 gem surges. Rig maneuvers are unchanged. Three maneuver slots and three surge sockets remain the tactical loadout limit. Existing gems retain their saved abilities; new gem rolls choose either variant within the same element/tier, retaining rarity and rank rules. Passive upgrades are unchanged.

## Presentation and tuning

Drill: compressed elbow windup into a straight piston extension, rail streaks. Sweep: torso counter-rotation into a broad lateral cut, segmented crescent. Erupt: lowered stance into a two-arm ground strike, rising shards. Cross: crossed forearms opening into a split release, double helix fragments. First-person motions follow the same release clock as third-person poses and server damage.

Effects are short-lived, distance culled, and bounded per release. Existing element impact sounds and hit confirmation remain in use. Damage values are weapon multipliers before rank, elemental defenses and area falloff. These are initial tuning values requiring PvP and three-player dungeon playtesting, not final balance certification.
