# Open raid expedition

The normal dungeon queue now deploys a ready party of 3–4 into a bounded, private open map. The owner Full test runs the same map solo without rewards; the Sahur test remains a focused boss test.

- A 520-stud-wide traversable field replaces sequential rooms and gates. Nine randomized camps surround an elemental Colossus clearing. Ore clusters, rocky outcrops, rim cliffs, bandit supplies and ancient monoliths mark the space.
- Six monster camps and three space-bandit camps contain 2–3 actors each. Monster gear follows its element and randomized camp rarity; bandits retain the rival bot's randomly generated player-style equipment with red tinting.
- Monsters wander and feed on ore, attacking within 32 studs. Neutral bandits watch within 11 studs (approximately ten feet), then resume wandering/mining. Damage provokes retaliation. Combat uses the existing rival AI, animations, gems and equipment rules.
- Bandits switch to a mining pick while mining. Monsters use a bent feeding/bite pose. Server-replicated poses are visible to the party.
- Enemies reset and recover if pulled beyond a 75-stud leash. A boss requires three living squad members for activation and damage, except authorized solo tests.

## Boss loot

After the boss falls, remaining actors are removed and eligible survivors have 30 seconds to Claim, Roll or Pass on one elemental Ultra Rare/Legendary Tier IV, Rank III gem. A sole claimant wins; multiple claimants/rollers compete on server-generated 1–100 rolls, with random tie-breaking. Pass/no response declines. Rolls do not spend currency or risk existing possessions. Full gem pouches receive 500 coins instead, in addition to the existing 300-coin completion reward. Test runs give no rewards.

Loot choices are validated against the run and eligible roster; repeat submissions and outside-party claims are rejected. Reward resolution runs once, and the instance closes five seconds afterward. The time limit does not interrupt an active loot decision.
