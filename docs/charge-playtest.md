# Earned Charge playtest

Start 0, capacity 100, no baseline passive regeneration. Landed health-damaging weapon hits grant 5 Charge (light) or 10 (heavy). Misses and fully absorbed hits grant no normal hit Charge. Existing conditional resource abilities remain.

Surge price bands: Common 20–30; Uncommon 30–40; Rare 40–55; Ultra Rare 55–75; Legendary 75–100. Within each band, the original ability price determines relative expense. Size/Clarity/Cut do not discount it. Ranked preserves existing normalization: both output and price use Common-equivalent rules. Existing ability damage scaling stays unchanged for this first cost playtest.

Cryo Stream pays upfront for up to two seconds. Early release or interruption does not refund Charge; continuing requires holding the cast input. Holding cannot extend the paid duration. Player UI and Rival bots use the same billing policy.

New randomly rolled helmet signatures (existing signatures are preserved):
- Conductor: +20–40% normal weapon-hit Charge; wearer stamina regeneration -15%.
- Momentum: every third damaging sword hit within a rolling five-second window yields 4–8 bonus Charge; wearer stamina regeneration -10%.
- Recycler: damaging sword hits activate 1–2 Charge/second for three seconds, refresh without stacking; wearer stamina regeneration -15%.
Only one helmet signature is active. Charge Gain and Countercharge remain available. Enchantments improve values within existing caps. No opponent stamina debuff.

## Studio playtest
Start a fresh Rival fight to reset resources. Confirm idle Charge remains zero, and successful light/heavy hits earn 5/10 without Charge perks. Equip Common and Legendary versions of the same Surge: compare time to first cast and usefulness per Charge. Check miss/block/absorb outcomes. Test a Legendary channel below its price, then with 100 Charge; hold it past two seconds. Test each helmet perk, its expiry, and stamina recovery while not guarding. Repeat with another player and under Ranked normalization.

Automated coverage verifies price bands for all 36 Surges/five rarities, upfront channel cost/expiry, armor triggers and non-stacking expiry, plus the combat regression suite. Human playtesting is still required to judge whether expensive Surges earn their cost; utility abilities may need separate payoff tuning.
