CHARGE 2.0 — EARN YOUR SURGES

LANDED HITS ONLY

Charge builds only when your attacks connect. The bigger the surge, the more of the bar it drinks. Aggression is the economy — you cannot turtle your way to a wall of ice.

STAMINA IS YOUR GUARD

Three consumers — heavy, block, dodge — one bar. Spend it all and your guard shatters open. Blocking everything is a choice with a bill; dodging everything is a budget. Manage the bar, win the fight.

DUELS — FIRST TO 2, PIVOT BETWEEN ROUNDS

MATCH FORMATFirst to 2 round wins, max 3 rounds. Sixty-second rounds in the starter bracket.
THE 30-SECOND PIVOTBetween rounds you may only swap to one of your 3 saved loadout presets — no mid-match editing. Both lock, then builds reveal.
SCOUTINGYou see the elements your opponent used in the round just played. That's the whole intel game — read the colors, counter-build.
NORMALIZED ARENAIn Ranked and Casual, Health, Charge and Stamina are fixed, all 9 sockets open regardless of level, passive augments off. Your gear and your reads are the edge.
OUTPOST CHALLENGESDirect duels both players accept — full progression counts. Consensual flexing: bring your level 40 chassis to the parking lot.
YOUR PRESETSThree builds, saved between fights. A preset is a full kit: armor, gems, the works. Composing them is half the game.
