ARMOR — PICK YOUR FAMILY

Three armor families, five slots, five rarities: 75 pieces, each with base stats and one permanent innate trait. No crafting — armor comes from bosses and the Market, full stop.

PROSPECTOR

The mining family. Sustain and utility: Stamina, Charge and HP recovery tied to combo hits, timed guards and clean dodges. Traits like Surveyor's Sense — land 3 alternating hits, restore Charge.

SKIRMISHER

The duelist family. Mobility and crit payoffs off flanks, dodges and perfect releases. For players who treat the arena like a fencing mat.

JUGGERNAUT

The wall family. Block-and-heavy payoffs: absorption, knockback reduction, threat and control. When the Juggernaut raises the shield, the fight asks you a question.

WHAT A PIECE CARRIES

BASE STATS + SET BONUS

Budget grows by rarity (10 / 15 / 22 / 30 / 40 points) into Health, Power, Defense, Speed and Crit. Wear 4 pieces of a family for the set bonus — the 2-piece and 6-piece tiers are retired.

ONE INNATE TRAIT, FOREVER

Every piece has a permanent, gem-independent trait with its own trigger — timed guards, clean dodges, alternating combos. Remove the gem, keep the identity. Higher rarities get juicier triggers.

TRIGGER VOCABULARY

Traits speak a shared language you learn once: timed guard (block at the last instant), clean dodge (i-frames confirm the avoid), alternating hits (weave your swings), flank (hit from behind). Sustain traits are capped and earned — no free healing exists.
