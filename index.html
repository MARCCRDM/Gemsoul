THE LOOP

Three verbs, endlessly. The mine feeds the fight, the fight feeds the market, the market feeds the mine.

⛏ MINE

Swing your pick at the vein. Charges cap at 50 and refill on their own, so every trip counts. Surface stones are common — depth is where the legends glow.

⚔ FIGHT

Landed hits fill your Charge bar. Spend it on surges, walls, and weapon specials. Aggression is the only path to your abilities — turtling starves you.

◆ OUTFIT

75 armor pieces, 9 sockets, 3 saved presets. Between duel rounds you get 30 seconds — read their elements, pivot your build, counter them.

TEN THINGS EVERY MINER KNOWS

  1. Your name is engraved on every gem you mine. Sell it, and your name travels with it — someone across the game is wearing a stone you found.
  2. Your leftover loot pays you later. Boss drops you leave behind stock the Market — and when yours sells, you collect a commission.
  3. 27 Commons become one Ultra Rare. Fusion turns your junk drawer into treasure through 13 fuses. The Legendary route needs a catalyst only bosses drop.
  4. Two gems of the same rarity are never equal. Size, Clarity, Cut — a flawless Common can outshine a lazy Rare.
  5. Elements collide. Thermal meets Cryo and shatters the fight open. Mixed kits trigger the reactions.
  6. Perfect timing is free power. Release your heavy attack on the beat and it refunds its own Stamina.
  7. Blocking at the last instant is a build engine. Timed blocks cost half and trigger armor traits.
  8. Levels don't make you stronger — gems do. Your level grows your chassis; your stones swing like legends.
  9. Go mono to become elemental. Matching Core gems attune you: extra resist and boosted stats.
  10. Win fights with your wallet-earned kit, not your stats. In the Arena everyone's normalized — build and skill decide.
