THE MARKET — RUN BY PLAYERS

There is no vendor. Every gem, every armor piece on the Market was mined or fought for by a real player — and the gems carry the miner's name.

SELL YOUR SURPLUS

Duplicate drops, wrong elements, lazy rolls — someone else's build is starving for exactly them. Listings that never sell expire and vendor at a floor price, so the market stays clean.

BUY YOUR EDGE

The Arena is normalized — but gear is the intended edge. PvE players supply, PvPers buy, miners scale. Everyone has a profession.

MINE TO ORDER

Deeper veins roll rarer stones. Mining Charges cap at 50 and refill 1 every 2 minutes — bring a plan, not a macro.

THE SINKS THAT KEEP IT HONEST

FUSIONCoins sink into upgrading stones — 3 into 1, 27 Commons into an Ultra Rare.
RECUTChange a stone's Kind without losing its quality. Priced above Fusion so it never becomes the cheap shortcut.
EXPIRED LISTINGSUnsold gear vendors out at a floor price — supply can't pile up forever.
COMMISSIONS ARE SELLER-FUNDEDDropper commissions come out of the sale, not minted from thin air. No inflation.

YOUR NAME, ON SOMEONE'S LEGENDARY

Mine deep, and the stones you sell carry your signature into other players' kits. The Market remembers who found what — first sale pays the finder's commission.
