THE FOUR ELEMENTS

Every gem, every armor drop, every boss belongs to one of four elements. Read the colors and you read the fight.

THERMALBOSS: TUNG TUNG TUNG SAHURWEAK TO CRYO

BURN & PRESSURE

The commitment element. Burns stack pressure over time — fight the Thermal boss and you learn to read windups or cook. On your sword: burn on hit. On your shield: blocked damage reflected back.

CRYOBOSS: TRALALERO TRALALAWEAK TO ION

FREEZE & CONTROL

The patience element. Chills slow everything they touch — including you. On your sword: slow on hit. On your shield: an absorb barrier that recharges.

IONBOSS: BOMBARDIRO CROCODILOWEAK TO CORROSIVE

CHAIN & INTERRUPT

The tempo element. Lightning chains between targets and interrupts. On your sword: hits arc to a second target. On your shield: a chance to negate a hit entirely.

CORROSIVEBOSS: BRR BRR PATAPIMWEAK TO THERMAL

ROT & SHRED

The attrition element. Corrosion makes everything take more damage. On your sword: targets take +5% damage from all sources. On your shield: cleanse a debuff on block.

THE COUNTER CYCLE

Each element beats the next — counters deal 25% more damage. There is no best element, only the right one for the round you just lost.

REACTIONS

Elements don't just stack — they collide. Mixed-element kits (your starter kit is always 2–3 elements, never mono) can trigger reactions when opposing effects meet. Frozen, then shattered. Burning, then rotted. The combo is the damage.

MIX & COLLIDE

Watch for the reaction window when two opposing element effects land on the same target.

READ THE COLORS

Opponent gear shows its element per lane. Scouting is literally "what color beat me?"

ATTUNEMENT

Stack matching Core gems in your armor and you attune to the element: 2 matching Cores = +10% resist. 4 matching Cores (Pure) = +20% resist and +10% to that element's stat — Power for Thermal, Vitality for Cryo, Haste for Ion, Precision for Corrosive. Attunement is the only resistance source in the game, so it's a real identity choice.
