THE BOSSES

One boss per element, each built to teach its element's language. Fighting the Frost boss is the Cryo tutorial nobody has to read.

THERMALWEAK TO CRYO

TUNG TUNG TUNG SAHUR

Commitment and pressure. Visible windups, punishing damage — the fight that teaches you to respect a telegraph.

CRYOWEAK TO ION

TRALALERO TRALALA

Control and tempo. Slows and freezes punish greedy approaches.

IONWEAK TO CORROSIVE

BOMBARDIRO CROCODILO

Chains and interrupts. Position badly and the whole room shares your pain.

CORROSIVEWEAK TO THERMAL

BRR BRR PATAPIM

Attrition and rot. Long fight, ticking stacks — cleanse or drown.

DUNGEONS

BOSS DUNGEONS

Solo or group, multiple difficulties. Group loot priority goes by contribution — the top contributors pick first.

PATHWAY DUNGEONS

3–4 players, a generated path of rooms, a hard time limit, and a Colossus at the end. Built for a squad night.

LOOT: PICK ONE

FIVE DROP. YOU TAKE ONE.

Every kill reveals a pool of 5 armor pieces, rarity-weighted and leaning toward the boss's element. You pick exactly one. The other four flow into the player-run Market — that's where the economy's armor stock comes from. First 3 pools a day grant picks; after that kills pay coins.

THE DROPPER COMMISSION

When a piece from your pool first sells, the pool's earners collect ~5% of the sale, split by contribution. First sale only — resale is nobody's business. Being a supplier is a profession: your kills keep paying after you log off.
