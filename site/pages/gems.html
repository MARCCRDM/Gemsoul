SAGA STONES — THE GEMS

Gems are the power in Saga Miners. Every one is mined by a player, engraved with the miner's name, and socketed into gear. There are no class skills to level — your stones are your build.

THE FOUR KINDS — ONE JOB EACH

A stone rolls its Kind at mining. Each Kind does exactly one thing, socketed in exactly one socket type.

● CORE — Stats

Socket in armor. Grants flat stats that feed every formula: Thermal=Power (surge damage), Cryo=Vitality (max HP), Ion=Haste (cooldown reduction), Corrosive=Precision (crit chance). Stack matching elements to Attune. One Core is mechanical (Size); the rest is collector detail.

⚡ SURGE — Abilities

Socket in Q, E or R. These are your actives: Q = Strike (offense), E = Guard (defense casts), R = Shift (movement). Sockets are role-locked — a Strike surge only fits Q. Big surges drink most of your Charge bar.

⚔ EDGE — Weapon Rider

Socket in your sword. Procs on hit, 20–30% chance by grade: Thermal burns, Cryo slows, Ion chains to a second target, Corrosive makes them take extra damage.

🛡 WARD — Shield Rider

Socket in your shield. Procs on block: Thermal reflects damage, Cryo raises an absorb barrier, Ion can negate a hit outright, Corrosive cleanses a debuff.

THE THREE GRADES

Every stone — no matter the Kind — is graded on the same three axes, with fixed meanings:

SIZE — MAGNITUDE

How big the numbers are. Damage, absorb, effect strength. A size-10 surge multiplies 45% harder than size-1.

CLARITY — UPTIME

Cooldowns and durations. Higher Clarity = your surges come back faster and your effects last longer (capped at 30% total with Haste).

CUT — TRIGGER

Proc chances. Edge procs run 20% +1% per Cut. On a Core this is collector flair, not mechanics.

RARITY & THE ROLL

Five rarities, and each rolls its grades inside its own band — so a perfect Common is a real perfect Common, not a sad number next to a Legendary.

RARITYGRADE BANDWHAT IT MEANS
Common1 – 4Everyday stones. Perfect rolls are worth keeping.
Uncommon2 – 6The workhorse tier.
Rare4 – 7Sets in after your first dungeon runs.
Ultra Rare6 – 9The fusion ceiling for mortals.
Legendary8 – 10Catalyst-gated. The mine sometimes gifts one.

Your card shows a Rarity badge and a Roll Quality grade (C/B/A/S) — graded against the stone's own band. A true 10/10/10 is a FLAWLESS, a distinct badge on top of S, whatever the rarity.

FUSION — TRASH TO TREASURE

3 → 1

Three stones of the same Kind and element fuse into one of the next rarity — and you choose which of the three effects survives. 27 Commons = one Ultra Rare, through 13 fuses.

THE LEGENDARY BRIDGE

Legendary isn't fusion's ceiling — it's a separate road: 5 Ultra Rares plus a catalyst that only boss supply drops carry. Or get absurdly lucky at the deep vein. Fusion prices and Kind odds are being finalized in the economy simulation.

MINING CHARGES

Swinging your pick costs Charges. Cap 50, free refill of 1 per 2 minutes (online or offline), and roughly 14 swings per stone on the current tuning. Coin refills exist for the impatient — priced in the simulation. Depth equals rarity: the surface is Common country; the deep veins are where the Market gets its stock.
